Security

Four proofs on every single tap.

A tap isn't trusted — it's verified. Each clock-in is scored against four independent signals before it lands in your records.

Proof of moment

One-time signature

The plaque's NTAG 424 DNA chip signs every tap with a fresh counter + AES cryptogram. Copied or replayed links are rejected — you must physically be at the plaque, right now.

Proof of person

Phone session

Identity lives in a secure session on each employee's own phone, behind their own screen lock. No shared PINs, no swappable cards, no buddy punching.

Proof of place

Site IP match

Requests from your site's WiFi carry its static IP — hard evidence the person is inside the building, and it resolves the location automatically.

Backup proof

GPS fallback

WiFi down or phone on mobile data? GPS confirms presence within 150 m. Anything unverified is stored, flagged, and queued for manager review — never silently approved.

Privacy by design: Tappa stores no fingerprints, no face scans, and never tracks location — GPS is read only at the moment of the tap. Built for GDPR from day one.

Questions, answered

The ones every ops manager asks.

What if someone doesn't have their phone that day?

The shift lead records the entry from the dashboard in ten seconds. It's stored with a "manually entered by" label, so your reports always show exactly which records were tapped and which were vouched for.

What if the phone has no NFC?

Every plaque also carries a QR code — the camera opens the same page. QR taps skip the cryptographic signature, so Tappa automatically requires the IP or GPS proof instead before approving.

Can someone clock in from home?

No. The plaque's chip signs each tap with a one-time cryptogram, so the link only exists if a phone touched the plaque at that moment. A copied link, a screenshot, or yesterday's URL is rejected by the server — and IP + GPS have to agree the phone is on site.

How do we migrate from our current device?

Import your staff list from a CSV export, hand out invite links, and run both systems side by side for a week. You compare the records, then unplug the old box. Most sites switch in one pay period.

Where does the data go?

Into your dashboard, then wherever you need it: daily CSV exports or a clean API feed your payroll or HR system directly. Tappa is the source of truth for in/out times — it doesn't try to replace your payroll.
