What this changes, and what nobody can tell you yet

Your copy does NOT switch the Taptime rule off. Until you do that, both are weighed together and the more specific one wins where they disagree — so a copy scoped to one venue changes that venue and leaves the rest as they were.

Records already written are not touched. Where a rule decided one, it names that rule — and if the rule was a stored one, your own or a Taptime baseline, it names the exact version too, which can never be edited or deleted. Where one of the Taptime guarantees decided there is no stored version to name, and a record you typed in by hand names the person who entered it rather than a rule. What changes here is how the NEXT tap is judged.

This release cannot tell you how many of last week’s taps would have come out differently — replaying past taps against a changed rulebook is a separate piece of work and it is not built yet. So treat this as a change you are making with your eyes open rather than one that has been checked for you.
