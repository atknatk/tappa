Touch has no hover, so it is left out here; focus-visible still applies to keyboards and switch access. The header nav links live in the Menu on a phone.

Focus ring — WCAG 2.2 focus appearance

One ring for every public control: 2 px solid, 2 px offset. Because of the offset the ring always sits on the surface, never on the control. Ink on porcelain and paper; paper on green and ink. This replaces the landing's 3 px saffron ring: saffron measures 2.32:1 on porcelain and 2.62:1 on paper, below 3:1.

Ink on porcelain14.32:1
Ink on paper16.17:1
Paper on green7.73:1
Paper on ink16.17:1

CSS: :focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--ring); outline-offset: 2px } · --ring = ink, and paper inside green/ink bands. Pressed = :active. Disabled exists only on form submits. Visited links keep their default colour (navigation, not reading lists).
