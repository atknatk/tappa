A queue at the gate, one tap each.

What if the phone has no NFC?

The same page opens from a QR code with the camera. A QR tap carries no cryptographic signature — no proof of a physical touch — so it needs your venue's network address to match before it's approved. A position on its own is not enough: without the address it's stored, flagged and queued for review.
